# Menu modifiers

Modifiers are extra choices on a menu item, such as size, sugar level, or toppings, with or without an extra price.

## Creating a modifier group

1. Open **Master data › Modifier groups**, then **Add group**.
2. Enter the **Group name**, for example *Sugar level*.
3. Turn on **Required** if the customer must choose, then set **Minimum** and **Maximum**. A maximum of 1 shows as a single choice.
4. Add options with **Add option**: name, **Extra price** (negative for a discount), and mark **Default** if it is pre-selected.
5. Save.

## Attaching it to a menu item

1. Open an *F&B menu* product in **Products**.
2. On the **Modifiers** tab, tap **Add group** and choose the group. The group order here is the order in the choice sheet.
3. Save the product.

## Kitchen items and notes

The product's **Modifiers** tab has a **Kitchen** section:

- **Send to kitchen ticket**: turn it off for items that are not cooked (bottled water, crackers). When you **Send to kitchen**, these items are left off the ticket and marked *Served* right away. If none of the new items are kitchen items, no ticket is created.
- **Allow free-text notes**: turn it off when the item cannot be customised. A kitchen note you already typed is not used for this item and is kept for the next one; the modifier sheet hides the note field.

## When ordering

On the Tables screen, an item with modifiers opens a choice sheet. Pick the options, set the quantity, add a kitchen note if needed, then **Add to bill**.

> [!note] When an ingredient runs out, mark the option *Sold out* in the modifier group. Old receipts keep a copy of the modifier name and price.
