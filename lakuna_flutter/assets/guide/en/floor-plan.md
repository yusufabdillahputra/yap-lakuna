# Arranging the floor plan

Besides the grid mode, tables can be placed on a picture of the room so staff can see where each table is.

![Arrange floor plan: the plan image in the background, tables can be moved.](floor-plan)

## Steps

1. Open **Tables**, then tap **Arrange floor plan**. This needs the *Store settings* permission.
2. Choose an area at the top. Add a new one with **+ Area** if needed.
3. Under *Layout mode*, choose **Floor plan**, then upload a floor plan image: a top-down sketch photo, an export from a floor plan app, or an architect's drawing. PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG, up to 5 MB.
4. Tap **Add table** and choose a shape (round, square, long, bar seat) and the number of seats. Tables that already exist in grid mode appear under *Not placed yet*.
5. Drag each table into place. Tables snap to a 20 px grid. Tap a table to change its name, seats, shape, size, and orientation.
6. Tap **Save layout**.

Use **Undo** and **Redo** after a wrong move, and adjust **Plan opacity** so the tables stand out.

> [!note] On the Tables screen the floor plan is shown with positions locked: staff can only pan and zoom. No picture? Keep using **Grid** mode.
