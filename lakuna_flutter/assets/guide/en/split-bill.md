# Split bill

Split bill divides one open bill between guests who pay separately. The bill stays open until every part is paid.

![A split bill shared evenly by three guests.](split-bill)

## Steps

1. On the **Tables** screen, open the bill and tap **Split bill**.
2. Choose how to split:
   - **By item**: tap a guest to give them one portion. Items with a quantity above 1 are split per portion.
   - **Split evenly**: the total is divided equally.
   - **Custom amount**: each guest pays a chosen amount.
3. Set the number of **Guests** (2–8).
4. Tap **Pay part** for the first guest and finish the payment. Repeat for the next guest.
5. When every part is paid, the screen shows *Bill paid* and the receipt is saved.

> [!note] Does a guest want to see their share first? Tap **Print bill** on the guest's card (**By item** or **Split evenly**). The guest bill lists their portions (or the whole order when split evenly), their share of the discount, service, and PB1 if any, then **GUEST A DUE**. Print it on the receipt printer or share it as a PDF; nothing is saved. In By item mode the button turns on once every portion has a guest. **Custom amount** has no per-guest bill; use **Print bill** on the table's bill instead.

> [!note] Does the last guest want to pay later? Once their part is the last amount left on the bill (the other guests have paid and no QRIS is waiting), the part's Pay screen shows **Credit / receivable** for staff allowed to manage receivables. Choose the customer, enter a down payment if any, and save: the bill is completed as one credit receipt in that customer's name, and the receivable is the part's amount minus the down payment. See [Credit sales](credit-sale). In **Custom amount** mode, credit is only offered when the amount equals the whole remaining bill.

> [!note] A part paid with static QRIS can be saved with **Save and wait for confirmation**. The number of waiting QRIS payments appears on Home until confirmed.

> [!warning] Once a part is paid, the split is locked. To change it, cancel the waiting QRIS parts first. **Cancel split** is only available before any part payment.

Service, PB1, *prices include tax*, and total rounding are locked when the first part is paid. If those settings change mid-split, the remaining guests are still billed at the old rates, so no guest absorbs the difference. If every waiting QRIS part is cancelled, the bill goes back to the latest rates.

All parts are recorded as payments on the same receipt, so reports and stock stay on one receipt.

The money for each part goes straight into the drawer summary of the cash session that received it, even before the bill is settled. If guest A pays at register K01 and guest B at K02, A's cash counts in K01's drawer and B's cash in K02's drawer. A session that received a part can only be closed once the bill is settled. Sessions on other devices can still be closed.

> [!note] If a split bill is voided before it is settled, each cashier refunds the part they received from their own drawer. Once the bill is settled, the sale can only be voided on the device that received every part; otherwise use **Return** in History.
