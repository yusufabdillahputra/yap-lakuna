# Split bill

Split bill divides one open bill between guests who pay separately. The bill stays open until every part is paid.

![A split bill shared evenly by three guests.](split-bill)

## Steps

1. On the **Tables** screen, open the bill and tap **Split bill**.
2. Choose how to split:
   - **By item**: tap a guest to give them one portion. Items with a quantity above 1 are split per portion.
   - **Split evenly**: the total is divided equally.
   - **Custom amount**: each guest pays a chosen amount.
3. Set the number of **Guests** (2–8).
4. Tap **Pay part** for the first guest and finish the payment. Repeat for the next guest.
5. When every part is paid, the screen shows *Bill paid* and the receipt is saved.

> [!note] A part paid with static QRIS can be saved with **Save and wait for confirmation**. The number of waiting QRIS payments appears on Home until confirmed.

> [!warning] Once a part is paid, the split is locked. To change it, cancel the waiting QRIS parts first. **Cancel split** is only available before any part payment.

All parts are recorded as payments on the same receipt, so reports and stock stay on one receipt.
