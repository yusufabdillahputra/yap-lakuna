# Receipts & printers

Receipts can be printed on a Bluetooth thermal printer, a network printer, or through the system print dialog, and can be shared as a PDF.

![The Receipt & printer settings.](receipt)

## Setting up this device's printer

1. Open **Settings › Receipt & printer**.
2. Under *This device's printer*, choose the printer type (see below).
3. Tap **Test print** to make sure the printer responds.

Printer types:

- **Bluetooth** (Android): pair the printer in the phone's Bluetooth settings first, then **Find printers** and **Choose**.
- **Network (LAN)**: enter the printer address, for example 192.168.1.50:9100, then **Use this address**.
- **System print dialog**: for USB printers or printers with a driver.

The printer is saved per device and is not shared with other devices.

## Kitchen printer

In the *Kitchen printer* card, choose **Network (LAN)** or **Bluetooth** for the printer in the kitchen, set the **Ticket paper width**, and tap **Test print ticket**. From then on the kitchen ticket prints on the kitchen printer every time you tap **Send to kitchen**. Choose **Same as receipt printer** or **Forget kitchen printer** to go back to printing tickets with **Print ticket** on the receipt printer.


## Bar printer

If drinks are made at a bar, set up a second printer in the *Bar printer* card:

1. Choose **Network (LAN)** or **Bluetooth**, enter the address or choose the printer, and set the **Ticket paper width**.
2. Under **Categories to the bar**, tap the menu categories made at the bar, for example *Drinks*. Sub-categories follow their parent.
3. Tap **Print test bar ticket**.

On every **Send to kitchen**, items from the bar categories print on the bar printer under the heading *BAR TICKET*, and the other items stay on the kitchen ticket. Both share one ticket number. The ticket dialog shows both tickets with their own print status; if the bar printer fails, tap **Reprint bar ticket**. Until a category is chosen, every item still goes to the kitchen. Choose **Not used** or **Forget bar printer** to stop using the bar printer.

## Branch receipt content

Set the **Paper width** (58 or 80 mm), the **Receipt header** and **Receipt footer** (up to 4 lines), and check the *Receipt preview*. Turn on **Print automatically after payment** for Bluetooth or network printers. Tap **Save**.

## Reprinting

Open **History**, choose a receipt, then **Reprint receipt**. Reprinted receipts are marked REPRINT.
