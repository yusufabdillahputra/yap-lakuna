# Sales & wholesale prices

The **Cashier** screen shows products by category on the left and the order on the right (on phones, the order is a bottom sheet).

![The Cashier screen with four products in the order.](sale)

## Steps

1. Make sure a [cash session](cash-session) is open.
2. Find a product with the *Search product or scan barcode* field, or tap it in the grid. USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners work right away.
3. If the product has several variants or units, pick one in the *Choose variant / unit* sheet.
4. Tap an order line to change the quantity, or use the plus/minus buttons.
5. Tap **Pay** to go to [payment](pay-cash).

## Wholesale prices

When the quantity reaches a wholesale tier, the unit price drops automatically. The order line shows *wholesale ≥ quantity* and the saving appears as **Wholesale discount** in the summary.

> [!note] When stock runs out and *Allow negative stock* is off, the product cannot be sold. Change it in [Store settings](store-settings).

Tap **Clear** to remove the whole order before payment.
