# Split payment

One order can be paid with several methods, for example part cash and the rest by QRIS.

![Part paid in cash, the rest by QRIS still waiting.](split-payment)

## Steps

1. On the Pay screen, choose **Split across methods**.
2. Choose the first method, enter the **Amount**, then tap **Add method** for the next one. The *Remaining* amount is shown below. For card and transfer, also fill in the **Card approval no.** or **Transfer reference** if you have one (optional, up to 40 characters).
3. For static QRIS, tap **Confirm** once the money arrives. A QRIS part stays *Waiting* until confirmed.
4. When nothing remains, tap **Complete payment**.

> [!note] Cash may appear only once, and change comes only from cash. A non-cash amount cannot exceed the remaining amount.

The receipt lists every method and its amount, with the approval number or transfer reference under each card or transfer line. To split a restaurant bill between guests, use [Split bill](split-bill).
