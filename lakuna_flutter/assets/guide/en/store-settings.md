# Store settings

**Settings** is split into sections. Store sections appear only for staff with the *Store settings* permission; *Appearance & language* is available to everyone.

![The Sales & stock settings.](store-settings)

## Settings sections

- **Store profile**: the business name. Business type and time zone are shown only.
- **This branch**: branch name, address, and phone shown in the receipt header. Branch and device codes cannot be changed.
- **Payment methods**: the [static QRIS](pay-qris) image and details.
- **Receipt & printer**: see [Receipts & printers](receipt-printer).
- **Tax & service** (F&B): service charge, PB1, and the *Prices include tax* option.
- **Sales & stock**: *Allow negative stock*, *Total rounding* (Rp 100 or Rp 500), *Manual discount needs manager PIN*, and *Transfer account* (e.g. `BCA 123-456-7890`, shown on the payment screen).
- **Data & backup**: see [Backup & restore](backup-restore).
- **Appearance & language**: Light/Dark/Follow system theme, language, and **Display size** Normal or Large.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** and choose a section in the left column (on phones, choose from the buttons at the top).
2. Change the fields, then tap **Save** in that section.

> [!note] Branch settings apply to every device in this branch. Printer and appearance are saved per device.
