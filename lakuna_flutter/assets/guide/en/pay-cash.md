# Cash payment & change

After tapping **Pay** at the Cashier, choose a payment method.

![Cash payment: IDR 50,000 received, change is calculated automatically.](pay-cash)

## Steps

1. Choose **Cash**.
2. Enter the **Amount received**, or tap **Exact amount** if the customer pays exactly. Change is calculated automatically.
3. Tap **Complete payment**.
4. On the *Payment successful* screen, tap **Print receipt** or **Share PDF** if needed, then **New transaction**.

## Other methods

- **Debit / credit card**: charged on a separate EDC terminal. The approval number is optional.
- **Bank transfer**: check the bank statement first. The transfer reference is optional.
- **Static QRIS**: see [Accepting static QRIS](pay-qris).
- **Split across methods**: see [Split payment](split-payment).
- **Credit / receivable**: see [Credit sales](credit-sale).

> [!note] If total rounding is on in [Store settings](store-settings), the total is rounded to the nearest Rp 100 or Rp 500 and a *Rounding* line appears on the receipt.
