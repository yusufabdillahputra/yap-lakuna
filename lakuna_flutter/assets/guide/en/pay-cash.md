# Cash payment & change

After tapping **Pay** at the Cashier, choose a payment method.

![Cash payment: IDR 50,000 received, change is calculated automatically.](pay-cash)

## Steps

1. Choose **Cash**.
2. Enter the **Amount received**, or tap **Exact amount** if the customer pays exactly. Change is calculated automatically.
3. Tap **Complete payment**.
4. On the *Payment successful* screen, tap **Print receipt** or **Share PDF** if needed, then **New transaction**.

## Other methods

- **Debit / credit card**: charged on a separate EDC terminal. The approval number is optional and is printed on the receipt.
- **Bank transfer**: check the bank statement first. The transfer reference is optional and is printed on the receipt. If a *Transfer account* is set in [Store settings](store-settings), the account number shows on the button and in the *Transfer to* box; tap **Copy** to copy it and send it to the customer. If the store has several accounts, each one shows on its own row with its own **Copy** button. Tap the account the customer picked (or tap **Copy** on its row, which also selects it): a check mark appears, and **Complete payment** turns on only once an account is selected. The destination account is printed on the receipt (*To: …*) and broken down in [Reports → Summary](reports), so you can match each bank statement. A store with one account does not need to pick.
- **Static QRIS**: see [Accepting static QRIS](pay-qris).
- **Split across methods**: see [Split payment](split-payment).
- **Credit / receivable**: see [Credit sales](credit-sale).

> [!note] If total rounding is on in [Store settings](store-settings), the total is rounded to the nearest Rp 100 or Rp 500 and a *Rounding* line appears on the receipt.
