# Returns & voids

A return takes back some or all items from a paid receipt. A void cancels a whole receipt from today. Neither deletes old data; both record new entries.

## Returning items

1. Open **History** and choose the receipt (filter by date, cashier, method, or status).

> [!note] Older receipt? Type the receipt number, the customer's name or phone, or the card approval no. / transfer reference in the **Search** field. Search covers all dates (up to the latest 100 receipts); the cashier, method, and status filters still apply. Tap a date pill to go back to the list by date, or **Date range…** to choose your own **From** / **To** dates (at most 366 days).
2. Tap **Return items**. Enter the return quantity per item, or **Return all**.
3. Choose a **Reason**. For *Other*, a note is required.
4. Choose the **Refund**: *Cash from drawer* (needs an open cash session), *Transfer*, or *Deduct from credit* for credit receipts.
5. Leave **Put items back in stock** on if the goods can still be sold.
6. Tap **Process return**.

## Voiding a sale

1. Choose the receipt in **History**, then tap **Void sale**.
2. Enter the **Void reason**.
3. If you lack the *Void receipts* permission, a permitted staff member enters their PIN in the *Manager approval* window.

> [!warning] Voids are only for today's receipts in the cash session still open on this device, and not for receipts that were returned. For other receipts, use a return.

Stock is restored and a voided receipt is left out of the session recap. The history list can be downloaded with **Export to Excel**.
