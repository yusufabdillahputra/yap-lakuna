# Returns & voids

A return takes back some or all items from a paid receipt. A void cancels a whole receipt from today. Neither deletes old data; both record new entries.

## Returning items

1. Open **History** and choose the receipt (filter by date, cashier, method, or status).

> [!note] Older receipt? Type the receipt number, the customer's name or phone (any format, e.g. `0812-3456`, `0812/3456`, or `+62 812 3456`), the card approval no. / transfer reference, or an item name (product name, variant name, or both in any order, e.g. `sugar`, `iced tea`, or `rice 5 kg` for *Rice Premium · 5 kg*), including F&B order modifiers, their group names, and item notes (e.g. `coffee extra shot`, `coffee topping`, or `no ice`), in the **Search** field. Spaces in item names don't have to match: `5kg` also finds *Rice 5 kg*. Item modifiers and notes appear in the receipt details (*+ Large, Boba*, *Note: no ice*). In the results list, each receipt shows the matching items under its number (*Matching items: Iced Coffee Latte Regular*), at most two names then *+N more*, with the parts of each name that match your keywords highlighted. With several words, every word must appear in the same item. Search covers all dates (up to the latest 100 receipts); the cashier, method, and status filters still apply. Tap a date pill to go back to the list by date, or **Date range…** to choose your own **From** / **To** dates (at most 366 days).
2. Tap **Return items**. Enter the return quantity per item, or **Return all**.
3. Choose a **Reason**. For *Other*, a note is required.
4. Choose the **Refund**: *Cash from drawer* (needs an open cash session), *Transfer*, or *Deduct from credit* for credit receipts.
5. Leave **Put items back in stock** on if the goods can still be sold.
6. Tap **Process return**.

## Voiding a sale

1. Choose the receipt in **History**, then tap **Void sale**.
2. Enter the **Void reason**.
3. If you lack the *Void receipts* permission, a permitted staff member enters their PIN in the *Manager approval* window.

> [!warning] Voids are only for today's receipts in the cash session still open on this device, and not for receipts that were returned. For other receipts, use a return.

Stock is restored and a voided receipt is left out of the session recap. The history list can be downloaded with **Export to Excel**.
