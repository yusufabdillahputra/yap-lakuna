# Expenses

The **Expenses** menu records operating costs other than buying stock, such as electricity, gas, or drinking water. Expenses appear in the profit & loss report.

## Before you start

Create expense categories in **Master data › Expense categories**, for example *Electricity*, *Wages*, or *Operations*.

## Recording an expense

1. Open **Expenses**, then **Record expense**.
2. Enter the **Description**, choose the **Expense category**, and enter the **Amount**.
3. Choose where it is **Paid from**: store cash, cash drawer (open session, today's date only), or bank account.
4. Set the **Date** and attach a receipt photo if you have one.
5. Tap **Save expense**.

## Voiding

Open the expense detail, tap **Void expense**, enter a reason, then **Yes, void**. If the money came from the cash drawer, it goes back to the drawer in the open session.

The *By category* summary shows totals per category for the chosen period. Tap **Export to Excel** to download the list.

> [!note] Cash out from the [cash drawer](cash-session) with *Also record as an expense* turned on also appears here.
