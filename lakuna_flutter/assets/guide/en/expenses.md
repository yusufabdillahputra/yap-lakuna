# Expenses

The **Expenses** menu records operating costs other than buying stock, such as electricity, gas, or drinking water. Expenses appear in the profit & loss report.

## Before you start

Create expense categories in **Master data › Expense categories**, for example *Electricity*, *Wages*, or *Operations*. Once a name is typed, the dialog asks **Discard changes?** before closing without saving.

## Recording an expense

1. Open **Expenses**, then **Record expense**.
2. Enter the **Description**, choose the **Expense category**, and enter the **Amount**.
3. Choose where it is **Paid from**: store cash, cash drawer (open session, today's date only), or bank account.
4. Set the **Date** and attach a receipt photo if you have one. Phone
   camera photos up to 20 MB work as they are; Lakuna shrinks them to
   2,000 px before saving.
5. Tap **Save expense**.

If you switch menus after entering a description, amount, receipt photo, or a different date without saving, Lakuna asks **Discard changes?** first. Choose **Save** to record the expense and go straight on, **Discard** to leave without saving, or **Cancel** to go back. If a field is missing, you stay on Expenses and the message appears under the field (on a phone, the form dialog opens again). On a phone the entry stays when you close the dialog with **Cancel**, so you are asked after closing the dialog too. The chosen category and payment source do not count, because they stay selected for the next expense.

## Voiding

Open the expense detail, tap **Void expense**, enter a reason, then **Yes, void**. If the money came from the cash drawer, it goes back to the drawer in the open session.

The *By category* summary shows totals per category for the chosen period. Tap **Export to Excel** to download the list.

> [!note] Cash out from the [cash drawer](cash-session) with *Also record as an expense* turned on also appears here.
