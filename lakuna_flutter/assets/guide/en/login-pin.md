# Signing in with a PIN & switching cashier

Every staff member signs in with a 6-digit PIN on the *Who's on duty?* screen. The owner can also sign in with email and password.

![Sign-in screen: pick your name, then enter your PIN.](login)

## Steps

1. Tap your name in the staff list.
2. Type your 6-digit PIN on the keypad.
3. To hand over mid-shift, tap **Switch cashier** in the header (an icon at the top right on phones), then choose the next staff member.

## Signing in as the owner

Tap **Sign in with email & password (owner)**, enter your email and password, then **Sign in**. This helps when the owner forgets the PIN.

> [!note] PINs are checked on this device. After 5 wrong attempts the staff member is locked for 1 minute. The owner or a manager can unlock sooner in [Users & roles](users-roles).

## Forgot your PIN

- **Staff**: ask the owner or a manager to open *Users & roles*, choose you, and enter a new PIN and repeat it.
- **Owner**: sign in with email and password, then change the PIN in **My account**.
