# Installation & owner account

Lakuna works offline: all store data is kept on this device. The first time you open it, the app asks you to set up the store in four steps.

![Home after the store is ready.](home)

## Before you start

- Install Lakuna from the installer for Windows, macOS, Linux, or Android.
- Have your business name, branch name, and owner email ready.

## Steps

1. **Language & theme**: choose Bahasa Indonesia or English, then the Light, Dark, or Follow system theme.
2. **Business type & branch**: choose *Retail*, *F&B*, or *Mixed*. Fill in the business name, branch name, branch code (2–6 letters/digits), device code, and time zone.
3. **Owner account & PIN**: enter the owner name, email, password and **Repeat password**, then a 6-digit PIN and **Repeat PIN**.
4. **First product**: choose **Add one product now** (enter the name, selling price, and optionally the cost price, unit, barcode, and opening stock), **Import from Excel/CSV** (the Products screen opens with the import dialog once the store is created), or **Skip, add later**. Review the summary, then tap **Start selling**.

> [!warning] Business type, time zone, branch code, and device code cannot be changed after setup because they decide the menu, report dates, and receipt numbers.

## After setup

- The branch and device codes appear in receipt numbers, for example PST-K01-261104-0001, so receipts never clash even when several cashiers work offline.
- *F&B* and *Mixed* businesses get the **Tables** menu for open bills.
- Continue with [Adding your first product](first-product) and [Cash session & drawer](cash-session).
