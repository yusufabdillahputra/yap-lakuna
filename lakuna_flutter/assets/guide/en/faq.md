# FAQ

## Does Lakuna need the internet?

No. Every Phase 1 feature works offline and the data is stored on this device. This guide is available offline too.

## Can two cashiers share the same data?

In the free version, data is per device. Each device has its own code in receipt numbers so they never clash. Syncing between devices comes with the [hosted version](hosting).

## I forgot my PIN.

Ask the owner or a manager to reset it in [Users & roles](users-roles). The owner can sign in with email and password, then change the PIN in **My account**.

## Why can't I sell a product that is out of stock?

*Allow negative stock* is off. Record a [purchase](purchases) or a [stock adjustment](stock), or turn the setting on in [Store settings](store-settings).

## The customer says they paid by QRIS, but the money has not arrived.

Do not tap **Paid** until the money shows in your bank statement or notification. See [Accepting static QRIS](pay-qris).

## How do I move my data to a new device?

Make a [backup](backup-restore) on the old device. Install Lakuna on the new device, complete the short setup, then restore the .zip file from **Settings › Data & backup**. The temporary setup data is replaced by the backup.

## Where is the app data stored?

The data location and device ID are shown under *Device info* on Home. Mention both when asking for technical help.

## Is the location in phone photos saved too?

No. Phone camera photos usually carry the GPS location, time, and phone model. When you save a product photo, invoice photo, receipt photo, payment proof, QRIS sticker, receipt logo, or floor plan image, Lakuna removes that data without changing the picture, so it never reaches backups or sync. Only the rotation is kept so the photo stays upright. Photos saved before version 0.72.0 are not changed; pick the photo again if you want to clean it.
