## Bahasa Indonesia (bawaan)

**Nama:** Lakuna – Kasir UMKM Offline

**Deskripsi singkat:** Aplikasi kasir gratis untuk toko & warung. Jalan tanpa internet, data di HP.

**Subtitle App Store:** Kasir toko & warung offline

**Kata kunci App Store:** kasir,pos,umkm,warung,toko,stok,qris,struk,restoran,kafe,laporan,offline

**Deskripsi lengkap:**

Lakuna adalah aplikasi kasir (POS) gratis untuk UMKM Indonesia: toko
kelontong, minimarket, warung makan, kafe, dan usaha F&B lain. Semua data
disimpan di perangkat sehingga kasir tetap jalan walau internet mati.

KASIR CEPAT
• Cari produk atau scan barcode, harga grosir otomatis
• Diskon per nota atau per item, dengan persetujuan PIN manajer
• Bayar tunai dengan kembalian, kartu, transfer, QRIS statis, atau gabungan
• Penjualan kredit (piutang pelanggan)
• Struk lewat printer Bluetooth atau jaringan, retur dan void tercatat

MODE F&B
• Meja dalam grid atau denah, open bill, kirim ke dapur
• Modifier (level pedas, topping) dan split bill per item atau bagi rata
• Service dan PB1 sesuai aturan daerah

STOK & PEMBELIAN
• Stok per lokasi berbasis kartu stok, stok minimum, penyesuaian dengan alasan
• Pembelian dari supplier, hutang supplier, dan retur pembelian

KEUANGAN & LAPORAN
• Sesi kasir dan kas laci, pengeluaran dengan foto nota
• Laporan penjualan, produk terlaris, laba rugi, unduh PDF dan Excel

TIM & KEAMANAN
• Staf masuk dengan PIN, role dan izin per fitur
• Backup dan pulihkan seluruh data toko dalam satu file

Tanpa iklan dan tanpa pelacak. Tampilan terang dan gelap, Bahasa Indonesia
dan English, panduan lengkap di dalam aplikasi. Bisa dipakai di HP, tablet,
dan komputer (Windows, macOS, Linux).

## English

**Name:** Lakuna – Offline POS

**Short description:** Free POS for shops, cafés and food stalls. Works offline, data on device.

**App Store subtitle:** Offline POS for small shops

**App Store keywords:** pos,cashier,point of sale,shop,cafe,restaurant,inventory,stock,receipt,qris,offline

**Full description:**

Lakuna is a free point of sale (POS) app for small businesses: grocery
shops, minimarts, food stalls, cafés and other F&B outlets. All data is
stored on the device, so the till keeps working when the internet is down.

FAST CHECKOUT
• Search products or scan barcodes, automatic wholesale prices
• Order or item discounts, with manager PIN approval
• Cash with change, cards, bank transfer, static QRIS, or split payment
• Credit sales (customer receivables)
• Receipts on Bluetooth or network printers, returns and voids recorded

F&B MODE
• Tables in a grid or on a floor plan, open bills, send to kitchen
• Modifiers (spice level, toppings) and split bills by item or evenly
• Service charge and local restaurant tax

STOCK & PURCHASES
• Stock per location with a stock card, minimum stock, adjustments with reasons
• Purchases from suppliers, supplier payables and purchase returns

MONEY & REPORTS
• Cash sessions and drawer cash, expenses with receipt photos
• Sales, best sellers and profit & loss reports, download as PDF or Excel

TEAM & SECURITY
• Staff sign in with a PIN, roles and permissions per feature
• Back up and restore all store data in one file

No ads and no trackers. Light and dark themes, Indonesian and English, and
a full in-app guide. Runs on phones, tablets and computers (Windows, macOS,
Linux).
